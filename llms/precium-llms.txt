Precium

Precium is a leading fintech company specializing in payment optimization and infrastructure solutions for enterprise businesses. Based in South Africa with global operations, Precium provides local payment processing capabilities, intelligent routing, automated reconciliation, and cross-border settlement services. The company focuses on helping businesses streamline payment operations, reduce churn, and enhance customer experiences through advanced payment technologies including network tokenization, smart routing, and multi-method processing capabilities.
